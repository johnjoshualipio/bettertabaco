# BetterTabaco — Product Requirements Document

**Product:** BetterTabaco  
**Proposed domain:** `bettertabaco.org`  
**Project type:** Independent civic transparency and public-information portal  
**Community:** BetterGov.ph / BetterLGU  
**Geographic scope:** Tabaco City, Albay, Philippines  
**Status:** Proposed / Pre-development  
**License goal:** Open source, with public-information content licensed where source terms permit  
**Cost to residents:** ₱0  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

BetterTabaco is a community-built, independent digital transparency portal for Tabaco City, Albay.

Its purpose is to make public local-government information easier to **find, understand, verify, and use**. It will organize fragmented information from official government websites, public documents, national open-data sources, and other authoritative public records into a fast, searchable, mobile-friendly portal.

BetterTabaco is **not an official website of the City Government of Tabaco** and must never present itself as one. It will complement—not replace—the official Tabaco City website and existing online services.

The initial portal will focus on:

- Government services and Citizen’s Charter information
- City offices and contact information
- Emergency hotlines
- Elected and appointed officials
- Barangay information
- Budgets and financial transparency
- Procurement and public bidding information
- Projects and programs
- Ordinances and resolutions
- Public documents and open data
- City statistics
- Tabaco history, culture, tourism, and the Tabak Festival
- Clear links to official online systems

The product should feel **professional, trustworthy, civic, calm, and local**. It should not resemble a political campaign site, tourism-only portal, or generic government template.

---

# 2. Background

BetterGov.ph maintains the BetterLGU initiative: a community of independently maintained transparency portals for Philippine local government units.

A BetterLGU portal is intended to make information such as budgets, projects, officials, ordinances, services, and contact information easier for citizens to access.

BetterTabaco will represent Tabaco City within that community.

At the time this PRD was prepared, BetterTabaco is still a proposed project and `bettertabaco.org` has not yet been registered.

# 4. Product Principles

## 4.1 Public information first

The portal exists to make information accessible, not to create another layer of bureaucracy.

## 4.2 Independent but respectful

BetterTabaco is community-maintained and independent from the City Government of Tabaco.

It should cooperate with official offices when possible while retaining editorial independence.

## 4.3 Source everything

Every important factual entry should identify its source.

Whenever possible, users should be able to open the original government page, document, dataset, or publication.

## 4.4 Explain, do not merely mirror

Government documents can be difficult to navigate.

BetterTabaco should turn public records into structured, readable information without changing their meaning.

## 4.5 Never impersonate government

The website must visibly disclose that it is an independent civic project.

It must not use language, seals, visual treatments, or domain structures that could reasonably mislead users into thinking the portal is an official Tabaco City government service.

## 4.6 Link to official transactions

BetterTabaco should not duplicate official application or payment systems.

For transactional services, the portal should explain the process and provide a clear **Continue to Official Service** action.

## 4.7 Mobile and low-bandwidth first

The site must work well on budget smartphones and slower Philippine mobile connections.

## 4.8 Accessible by default

Accessibility is a core product requirement, not an enhancement.

## 4.9 Politically neutral

The portal must focus on public institutions, records, services, and verifiable information.

It must not endorse candidates, political parties, or elected officials.

## 4.10 Maintainability over novelty

A simple portal that remains accurate for years is more valuable than a technically impressive portal that becomes stale.

# 6. Core User Problems

## Problem 1 — Public information is fragmented

Residents may need to visit several different pages, PDF files, social accounts, or online systems to understand a single transaction.

### Product response

Aggregate and structure information while linking directly to official sources.

---

## Problem 2 — Government terminology is difficult to navigate

Citizens often know what they want to accomplish but do not know the responsible office.

### Product response

Organize services by citizen intent instead of expecting users to understand LGU organizational structure.

Examples:

- Start a business
- Get a birth certificate
- Pay local taxes
- Apply for a building permit
- Find health services
- Request social assistance

---

## Problem 3 — Public documents are difficult to search

Budgets, ordinances, procurement notices, and other records may exist but are difficult to discover.

### Product response

Create searchable indexes with metadata and direct source links.

---

## Problem 4 — Information can become outdated

Government officials, contact numbers, fees, requirements, and documents change over time.

### Product response

Every structured record should include:

- Source
- Source publication date when available
- Last verified date
- Last updated date
- Data maintainer or change history where appropriate

---

## Problem 5 — Residents cannot easily judge source reliability

### Product response

Use visible source labels such as:

- Official City Government Source
- National Government Source
- Commission on Audit
- Philippine Statistics Authority
- Sangguniang Panlungsod
- Other Public Record

- Last verified
- Last updated

# 37. Technical Direction

BetterGov does not require one framework.

BetterTabaco should prioritize:

- Open source
- Low hosting cost
- Static-first rendering
- Excellent SEO
- Easy contribution
- Easy deployment
- Long-term maintainability

## Recommended MVP direction

A modern Vue/Nuxt-based public portal is a strong fit because most content can be statically generated while still supporting rich search and filtering.

Alternative React-based BetterLGU templates may also be used.

A Laravel backend is **not required for MVP** unless BetterTabaco later needs:

- A dedicated editorial CMS
- Automated ingestion workflows
- Complex APIs
- Moderation workflows
- Contributor accounts
- Database-backed administration

Avoid adding a server simply because it is familiar.

# 40. Non-Goals

BetterTabaco MVP will **not**:

- Replace the official Tabaco City website
- Claim to represent the City Government
- Accept taxes or government payments
- Issue permits
- Issue certificates
- Maintain citizen profiles
- Store government IDs
- Handle complaints that require official government case management
- Become a political campaign platform
- Rank politicians
- Publish allegations or unverified claims
- Duplicate government databases unnecessarily
- Operate as a commercial advertising portal

# 44. Success Metrics

BetterTabaco should measure usefulness rather than vanity traffic.

## Availability

- 99.9% public-site uptime target
- Zero dependency on a single contributor’s local environment

## Information Quality

- 100% of high-impact structured records have a source
- 100% of emergency contacts have a verification date
- 100% of services identify their responsible office
- 100% of financial visualizations link to underlying source data
- Less than 2% known broken external links
- Quarterly review of high-priority civic information

## Usability

- Common service reachable within 2–3 interactions from homepage
- Search results useful for common citizen-language queries
- Fully usable on mobile
- No login required for public information

## Performance

- Strong Core Web Vitals on representative mobile devices
- Homepage kept lightweight
- Search remains responsive with growing datasets

## Community

- At least two maintainers when practical
- Public contribution guide
- Transparent change history
- Issues addressed on a predictable cadence

# 45. Data Governance

## High-Risk Civic Information

Changes to the following should receive enhanced review:

- Emergency phone numbers
- Fees
- Eligibility requirements
- Service deadlines
- Processing times
- Elected officials
- Financial figures
- Procurement awards

## Conflict Rule

When BetterTabaco data conflicts with an official government source:

1. Treat the official source as authoritative unless there is compelling evidence it is obsolete or erroneous.
2. Flag the BetterTabaco record.
3. Correct it promptly.
4. Preserve the change history.
5. Do not editorialize the discrepancy.

---

# 46. Editorial Standards

Content should be:

- Neutral
- Concise
- Source-backed
- Written for ordinary residents
- Free of political praise or criticism
- Clear when data is incomplete
- Clear when historical information is folklore
- Explicit about dates

Avoid words such as:

- Best
- Most successful
- Transformative
- Visionary
- World-class

unless they are part of a clearly attributed source.

# 52. Future Vision

BetterTabaco can evolve from a directory into a durable civic information infrastructure for Tabaco City.

The long-term opportunity is not to create more government software.

It is to create a **clear public layer over information that already belongs to the public**.

A successful BetterTabaco should allow a resident to arrive with a simple question and leave with:

1. The information they need
2. The official source
3. The correct government destination
4. Confidence that the information can be verified

The project should remain independent, open, politically neutral, accessible, and free to the public.
